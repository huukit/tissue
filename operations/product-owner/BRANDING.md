# Tissue — Branding

Working file for slogan and visual identity. Cheeky, dev-tool register. Names the pun at the heart of the product: *tissue* (the thing that cleans up) contains *issue* (the thing we track).

## Slogan

**Primary:**

> **Tissue — wiping AI's ass with the help of humans.**

Honest tagline of the joke: AI creates the mess, humans stay in charge of the cleanup. That *is* the product.

**Alternatives / secondary lines:**

- "Tissue — every issue has a clean side." *(the dual-format model, as a double entendre)*
- "Tissue — for when your AI makes a mess of your issues."
- "Tissue — AI makes the mess. Humans keep it clean."
- "Tissue — soft on humans, tough on AI output."
- "Got issues? Wipe responsibly." *(campaign tagline, not primary)*

## Logo

**Concept:** a tissue box (square, with the oval dispensing slot) viewed slightly from above, with a single tissue sheet pulled out. Inside the dispensing slot, a checkmark — the sheet emerging *is* approval. Human hand approves, the sheet absorbs the AI mess.

**Elements:**
- The box reads as a rounded square = repository/storage.
- The pulled sheet with a ✓ = the approved human-format proposal.
- Optional: small sparkles on the sheet standing in for the AI's voluminous output being absorbed.

**Draft:** `logo.svg` in this directory.

**Palette (draft):**
- Ink: `#1C1B22`
- Box body: `#F4F1EA` (paper)
- Slot + accents: `#FF6B5E` (coral — the "AI" energy)
- Approval ✓: `#2E7D5B` (green — human-approved state)
